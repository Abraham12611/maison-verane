# Attribution

Maison Verane is a fictional brand. It is not a real business and has not been trademark-cleared.

## Logo and illustrations (vector)

Generated with Recraft V4.1 Text to Vector on fal (`fal-ai/recraft/v4.1/text-to-vector`): the wordmark, the V monogram, the lockup, the "Le Voyageur" line drawing and the craft icons. `scripts/process-brand.mjs` removes the background rectangle and C2PA metadata and tightens each viewBox.

## Photography

Generated with GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst on fal (`openai/gpt-image-2.6/sunburst/text-to-image`): hero, four products, atelier, leathers, journey and hotel. The hero, hotel, atelier, leathers and journey images were upscaled 2x with SeedVR2 (`fal-ai/seedvr/upscale/image`). All images are synthetic. No real people or products are shown.

## Player

ÉCRIN · Voyage, Apache-2.0 from https://github.com/Abraham12611/ecrin. Leather texture and brass hardware in `public/assets` come from that project.

## Film

The atelier film is a third-party promotional video, supplied by the project owner and streamed from Mux. It is not made by or for this fictional brand, and a different maison's name is visible in some frames. It is used for demonstration only. Replace the playback ID in `src/app/BrandPlayer.tsx` before any public or commercial use.
